## Concept angle

A tiny illustrated macOS help card: warm and reassuring, with an oversized
pointer and a small cat signature so the first-launch workaround feels friendly
instead of alarming.

## Composition

- Focal element: a large privacy-safe Finder window or confirmation dialog on the right.
- Edge anchor: numbered instruction and short explanation on the left.
- Supporting detail: three-step progress rail, compact trust reminder, cat-ear geometry.
- Background treatment: warm cream field, coral radial glow, sparse rounded linework.

## Typography

- Archivo Black at weight 400 for large instructional headlines.
- IBM Plex Mono at weights 400 and 700 for labels, helper copy, and interface metadata.
- Headline sizes 82-116px; body 28-36px; labels 20-24px; minor Finder metadata 18-22px.

- System accent: `#D7A644`.

## Shape, spacing, and depth

- Corners: 12-34px across controls, menus, panels, and Finder windows; status chips may be pill-shaped and icons may be circular.
- Spacing: compact interface gaps use 8-28px; composition-level padding and gaps use 84-110px.
- Depth: layered but restrained, using one soft panel shadow and one low-opacity radial glow per scene.

## Motion

- Decisive pointer glides and synchronized press reactions.
- Smooth horizontal push transitions, with one vertical push at the confirmation step.
- One restrained ambient glow per scene; no perpetual wobble or decorative looping.

## What NOT to do

- No personal Finder paths, usernames, downloads, or desktop screenshots.
- No dark cyber-security styling, neon gradients, or fake warning drama.
- No tiny web-sized text, generic card grids, or excessive bounce.
- No narration, music, or sound effects.
