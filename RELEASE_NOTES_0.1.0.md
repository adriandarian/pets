## Pets Public Beta

The initial release of Pets brings your code companions to the Mac desktop, tracking your Claude and Codex sessions in real time as you work.

### What's new

- **Desktop companions**: Customizable digital pets that live on your desktop and evolve with your coding activity.
- **Session tracking**: Pets track Claude and Codex chat sessions, showing active work with visual indicators.
- **Pet customization**: Choose pet name, appearance, and positioning on screen.
- **Collection system**: Unlock new pets and accessories through the Collection rewards system as you use Claude and Codex.
- **Preferences**: Configure tracking behavior, notification preferences, and appearance settings.
- **First pets included**: Includes the Puppies family—Biscuit, Chip, Cocoa, Fudge, Honey, and Peanut—with full animation sets covering idle, busy, waiting, excited, and sleeping states.
- **Common, Rare, and Legendary rarities**: Collect variants of your favorite pets at different rarity levels through the chest system.

### How it works

Pets monitors your Claude Code and Codex sessions and awards Collection rewards based on observed usage. As you accumulate rewards, you unlock new pets and cosmetic items to customize your experience.

### Installing the update

Download `Pets-0.1.0.zip` and extract `Pets.app`. Move it to your Applications folder. Launch Pets and grant the necessary accessibility permissions when prompted.

Pets is signed with the maintainer's Apple Development certificate and hardened runtime, but it is not Developer ID signed or notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, Control-click `Pets.app`, choose **Open**, and confirm.

### Known limitations

- This is a public beta release. Report issues and feedback at https://github.com/adriandarian/pets/issues.
